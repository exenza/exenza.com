Throwing my arms at you
for a hug you didn't want to.

Love can't be given nor received.

A bright star, shining next to you,
light, that never made you shine.

A black hole devouring love, crashing in your core.
Prisoner of immense pressure to love your own.

Love can't be given nor received,
must be shared and my star is bright.

Rest in your darkness, you tried to do right.
