title: Always right
date: 2024-04-03
description: A poem about following directions while losing yourself.
image: /assets/img/stories/always-right.jpg

I'm always right
in the wrong way

Look, I always do
as you say

I'm always right
in the wrong way

Doing everything I can
but never doing it my way

Maybe is wrong to step away?
I can't hear what you say.

Going left and right
I will find a way.
