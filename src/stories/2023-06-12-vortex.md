Swooshing in a Vortex
So deep, by the end, I'm again on its surface.

Catching my breathing before my next drowning.
Plunging and drowning
Dreaming and falling.

Sun on my face, breeze on my head
Really alive or just dead?

Alive and well as the pain gain its momentum
swooshing in a Vortex
So deep, by the end, I'm again on its surface.

Let me rest deep under the sea
let me see for how long I can't breathe.
