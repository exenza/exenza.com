No passions, just obsessions
fueled on adrenaline
burning rage

I've built this cage
I've built this cage

Digging a tunnel
escaping myself

Black coal and dirt
no escape.

A thud on a locked chest
Deep breaths

The hearth is pumping
the diaphragm expanding

Burning hope
fueled by oxygen
No obsession just compassion
