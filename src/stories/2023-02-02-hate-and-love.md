---
title: Hate and Love
date: 2023-02-02
description: Hate conceals weakness; love carries the strength of kindness.
image: /assets/img/stories/hate-and-love.jpg
storyType: poem
---

The one who hates
conceals weakness with violence

The one who loves
carries the strength of kindness

Hate are the paths blindly followed by a torrent
Love is the strength of water carving the rocks.

The power of water is not its tumultuous flow,
torrents, with time, will be calm rivers carrying life.

So Love shall be chosen as hate is no choice at all
and when the sun rises, the calm river shall shine.
