description: A poem about running your own circus until the show has to end.

In a circle
Running your own circus

Lions in their cages
Showing off their pride

Ponies on the stage
Offered for a ride

In a circle
Running your own circus

Jumping through the hoops
Swinging up on high

Who will catch me
If I fall and die?

In a circle
Running your own circus

Clowning for laughter
Cry backstage, shortly after

Show must go on!
Show must go on!

In a circle
Running your own circus

Acrobats in motion
Crowd cheering with devotion

Should the show go on?

Shut the lights, leave the tent.
The show has come to its end.
