In a circle  
Running your own circus.

Lions caged in their pride,  
Pony horses for a ride.

In a circle  
Running your own circus.

Jumping hoops,  
swinging at heights.  
Hope the safety net has been fasten tight

In a circle  
Running your own circus.

Clowning for laughter,  
cry backstage, shortly after.

Show must go on!

In a circle  
Running your own circus.

Crowds are cheering, grinning monkeys.  
Lions are yawing, acrobats weaving.

"Good bye, until next time!"  
They shout off their lungs.

In a circle  
Running your own circus.

Show must go on?

Shut the lights, leave the tent.  
The show has come to its end.
