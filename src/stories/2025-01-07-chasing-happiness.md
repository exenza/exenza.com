---
title: Chasing Happiness
date: 2025-01-07
description: A poem about standing still after chasing too fast, too far.
image: /assets/img/stories/chasing-happiness.jpg
storyType: poem
---

I was chasing happiness
Now I'm standing still

Floating in a rocket ship
Traveling at light speed

I was chasing happiness
Flying to outer space

Zooming through the galaxies
Searching signs of life

I was chasing happiness
Now I'm flying home

Descending in the atmosphere
Landing on my lawn
