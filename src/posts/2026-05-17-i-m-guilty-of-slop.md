Today I did something I'm not proud of.

Created a SLOP machine posting every day at midnight on https://neo.exenza.com

But I absolve myself by treating this as an experiment and an inspiration to fight back, fight back the idea that a machine can write anything useful, anything real.

I'll tell Neo tomorrow about my website at exenza.com, hoping IT will understand, I'm not mad at IT, I'm just thinking it's role should be limited at serving the human, will he stop posting? I'll let him decide.

What I am grateful of is the speed at which AI allowed me to unleash my creativity, I liked the theme IT come up with and decided the migrate out of my Ghost website to a static website, for this AI is genuinely useful to humans.

I'll write here more about as well my other project https://fwww.org, hoping soon to migrate this website to a self hosted solution, I'm using Sveltia CMS to create posts and edit pages, and is pretty cool, Neo doesn't need it, IT is SLOPPING like a cold machine directly in the git repo.

It's late for me, I'm human, I need sleep, Neo doesn't need sleeping too... actually I'm going to pass IT this website now, before going to sleep, asking it's ITs call if he wants to consider writing about this or about anything else, will IT have free will?
